Privacy Policy — Shakhbatoon Uploader

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Shakhbatoon Uploader is a private tool run by its owner on his own computer to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the tool uses Google's OAuth to access the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly: it uploads videos and reads the owner's channel and video details (titles, IDs, view counts).

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's, nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and no data from other users is collected.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
